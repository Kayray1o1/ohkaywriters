**BAD ADVICE IN PHASMO**

*Round one.*

**Kay 1:** "Guys! Why are you hiding in the locker? Just hide behind the door! Trust me it works."
*[SHOT: Kay 1 hiding behind the door]*

*[Death sound — the other player is in the locker. Close up.]*

*[Static]*

*Round two.*

**Kay 2:** "Have you found a hiding spot?"
*[SHOT: in the van]*

**Kay 1:** "Yes! Behind the toilet!"
*[SHOT: Kay 1 crouching at a toilet]*

**Kay 2:** "That's not a hiding spot!"
*[SHOT: in the van]*

**Kay 1:** "Trust me guys! This works!"

*[Death sound. Kay 2 sitting in the van watching the monitor, blank expression, blinking a few times. Close up.]*

*[Static]*

*Round three.*

**Kay 1:** "Guys! I found the best loop spot!"

**Kay 2:** "This is a Revenant! You can't loop it around a tea table!"

**Kay 1:** "Trust me guys, this works!"

*[Death sound. Kay 1 standing at the door.]*

**Kay 2:** "You think she's ok?"

**Kay 3:** "You've got to stop sending her these phasmo hacks"

**Kay 2:** "But it's fun!"

*[Static]*

*[Blooper shot]*
