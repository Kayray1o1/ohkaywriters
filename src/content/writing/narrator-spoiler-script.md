*[Character drinking tea.]*

**Narrator:** Here is Emily, drinking her tea. Everything in her life is perfect

**Emily:** Yes it is, thank you very much

**Narrator:** It is such a shame she is the main character --

**Emily:** Ok....

**Narrator:** So in two days, she is going to lose anyone and everything she ever cared about

**Emily:** What

**Narrator:** Only to fall in love with a dark mysterious stranger

**Emily:** Turn it back, you said some about lose anyone and everything

**Narrator:** And get in a forced marriage with

**Emily:** I genuinely don't care about that, what was that about lose anyone ---

*[Static]*
