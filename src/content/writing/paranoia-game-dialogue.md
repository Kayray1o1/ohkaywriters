## 1. Scene 2: The Voice on the Radio

*A long corridor outside the cell door. Clark has just found a radio.*

**CLARK TURNER:** "Hello? How do I use this thing..."

**BRANDON B. WILLIAMS:** "Hello? Can you hear me?"

**CLARK TURNER:** "There we go." *[Beep]* "Yes, loud and clear."

**BRANDON B. WILLIAMS:** "Now don't freak out."

**CLARK TURNER:** "Where the hell am I? Who are you, and why is my head pounding?"

**BRANDON B. WILLIAMS:** "Okay, this is strange, I know. But you have to listen to me..."

**CLARK TURNER:** "How about you tell me something that's ACTUALLY useful?"

**BRANDON B. WILLIAMS:** "Dude, this is NOT the time to be an ass..."

**CLARK TURNER:** "Well, that was rude."

**BRANDON B. WILLIAMS:** "It's hard to explain everything. But I've been through all this before, and I can get you out of here alive."

**CLARK TURNER:** "Uh-huh. Sure."

**BRANDON B. WILLIAMS:** "You're welcome to carry on by yourself. It's not as if this place is a death trap, my friend."

**CLARK TURNER:** "I'm not your friend... but I'm listening."

**BRANDON B. WILLIAMS:** "Finally. Gosh. Let's just start by getting you through those doors, okay? The code is..."

## 2. VeilCore Employee Logs: Max Parks and Veronica Miles

*Audio logs the player finds in the sewer system. They double as survival hints for the area.*

**Max Parks** is the foundation's "prodigy": brilliant at identifying anomalies and creatures of the unknown, a quick study with survival skills, a typical nerd with a very soft spot for people. **Veronica Miles** is his assistant. She files the paperwork Max forgets, she's blunt and outgoing, and she secretly enjoys working for him.

### Employee Log 01

**MAX:** "I don't understand how this works."

**VERONICA:** "You just talk into the mic, and it records and transcribes it for you."

**MAX:** "What do you mean? It's not doing anything."

**VERONICA:** "How can you be a genius in your field and so dumb with technology? It's already transcribing. Look!"

**MAX:** "Oh. Thank you, Veronica."

**VERONICA:** "Uh-huh."

**MAX:** "So, I've been promoted to V3. According to the department, that means I can't work on the Subjects in the lower sewer section anymore. Which is a shame, because I was hoping to learn more about the water elements of Subject 442."

**VERONICA:** "I'm not complaining. It stinks here, and I have to go wherever you go."

**MAX:** "Yeah, that's because you're my assistant and I pay you. Do you mind? I'm trying to leave some, hopefully life-saving, notes for whoever gets assigned here next."

**VERONICA:** "Sorry. My bad."

**MAX:** "Okay, I've completely lost my train of thought. How do you stop this recording?"

**VERONICA:** "By pushing the big fat red button right in front of you, buddy."

**MAX:** "Which button? I don't see... what is it..."

### Employee Log 02

**MAX:** "Attempt number two, because my assistant is now on a coffee run, even though the coffee machine is broken. However long it takes her to discover that is how long I have to speak.

The water in the main section is essentially Subject 442's territory. It's mostly harmless, but it's not fond of anything disturbing the water. So take the bridges, and don't jump in. Besides, the water isn't exactly sanitary. If the Subject doesn't finish you off, the bacteria might. Fortunately, you should be issued the standard VeilCore gumboots. Did you know, by the way, that gumboots were invented in the early 1800s by Arthur Wellesley, the first Duke of Wellington? The same man who defeated Napoleon at the Battle of Waterloo? I find this fascinating because..."

**VERONICA:** *(distant)* "Maaaaax! The coffee machine is broken! What are we gonna do?!"

**MAX:** "That's my cue. Where's that stop button again?"

### Employee Log 03

**MAX:** "Subjects I understand. Subjects follow patterns. It's why I'm in this job. But people? People have always been a bit of a paradox to me..."

**VERONICA:** "Don't tell me you find the Subjects better company than people."

**MAX:** "Well, there are exceptions, of course. But I think it's good that people treat these Subjects with a healthy amount of respect."

**VERONICA:** "Because you're obsessed with them?"

**MAX:** "Because it keeps you alive. Staying out of the water is respecting Subject 442's space."

**VERONICA:** "What about Subject 443?"

**MAX:** "That one, you need a *very* healthy amount of respect for."

**VERONICA:** "No kidding. I have a heart attack every time you go in there."

**MAX:** "I was trying to make a log here, and you've completely sidetracked me... again."

**VERONICA:** "Whoops. To be fair, it won't hurt for people to know that anyway."

**MAX:** "True. Have you packed?"

**VERONICA:** "Everything's packed. The only thing I'm a little sad about is the greenhouse."

**MAX:** "I'll build you a new one."

**VERONICA:** "No need. Where we're going, we'll actually have some sunshine. For once. I swear, in another life you were a cockroach, with how much you love these sewers."

**MAX:** "I wish. The idea that cockroaches survive a nuclear bomb is a myth, you know. They wouldn't survive the direct blast. But they can survive six to fifteen times the radiation a human can. A human survives something like 400 to 1,000 rads..."

**VERONICA:** "Okay, buddy. You've completely lost me. And you're still recording."
