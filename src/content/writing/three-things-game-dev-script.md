I don't know what I was thinking when I decided to become a game developer *[SHOT: me standing and scratching my head]*

And honestly, I still don't know what I am thinking as I continue to develop *[SHOT: me standing and looking at the camera and speaking]*

Cause it's fun and apparently I love pain

So here are three things I wish I knew before I started game development *[SHOT: me showing three fingers in Paranoia]*

So firstly, I am not an organized person, but in game development, it's a must *[SHOT: me picking up papers scattered around]*

Because I promise you that you will hate yourself if you don't file things properly and waste so much time looking for things *[SHOT: mindmap and show of files]*

Two -- audio -- you have to add every single sound -- and yes, call it obvious, until you realize just how much things make noise *[SHOT: audio pixabay]*

The door opening, footsteps, if water is leaking from a tap, if a box falls. All those sounds you have to add in yourself or else it's going to make for an awkward game -- and it is a tedious process *[SHOT: me carrying a black bag while walking up a hill]*

And third -- game development is 10% design and 90% fixing bugs *[SHOT: me running from a bug]*
