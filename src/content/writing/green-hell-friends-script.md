*[Kay 1 sitting next to a fire, drinking soup. Kay 2 walks in.]*

**Kay 1:** Hey welcome back! here's some soup

**Kay 2:** Ah, no thanks, I already had three slugs, two grapes, one banana, some maggots -- that didn't taste very good, some macaw meat, cane toad meat, also a coconut and three or four suspicious looking mushrooms.

**Kay 1:** So just an average day

**Kay 2:** Pretty much. Now, I want to make some bone soup. Can't wait to drink that poisoned dart frog soup that I just made earlier today

**Kay 1:** The what now?

**Kay 2:** yeah -- the soup, you did read the stats of what you're drinking right?

*[Kay 1 stares at the soup.]*

**Kay 2:** RIGHT ... KAY. You did actually look at what you decided to drink...right?

**Kay 1:** Why would you make a soup like that?

**Kay 2:** I DON'T know! Thought it would be fun to make a very poisonous soup!

**Kay 1:** WITHOUT TELLING ME

**Kay 2:** You can read what's in the food before eating them!

**Kay 1:** I DIDN'T THINK THAT POISON SOUP WAS SOMETHING I HAD TO BE WORRIED ABOUT!

**Kay 2:** You always do this, go ahead eating and drinking stuff without thinking!

**Kay 1:** WELL I

*[Church bells ring.]*

**Kay 2:** Gonna miss you buddy
